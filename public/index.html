How Book Brewery brews
  1. Split. The book is broken into chapters using its own structure (EPUB TOC) or heading heuristics.
  2. Steep. Each chapter is analyzed by an educational-content persona — theme, core concepts in plain language, key terminology, frameworks, and inline diagrams for anything visual.
  3. Serve. After every chapter you can ask a follow-up, expand a section, or move on to the next.
