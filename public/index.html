How Book Brewery works
  1. You upload the ebook. We split it into chapters using the book's own structure (or heading heuristics).
  2. For each chapter, an educational content analyst persona produces a structured summary: theme, core concepts, key terminology, frameworks, and visuals as inline HTML.
  3. After every chapter you're asked whether to expand a section, ask a follow-up, or brew the next chapter.
